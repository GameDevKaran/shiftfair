Hard constraints stay hard

For each person and shift: mark availability, choose an inconvenience weight from 0–10, or lock a required assignment. A lock never overrides availability or hour limits.

How burden is countedHours × (1 + inconvenience weight), added across a person's shifts. We minimize the highest individual burden first, then squared burdens, then total burden. Weights are your choices, not inferred personal facts.
